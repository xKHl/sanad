### Error analysis

| Step | Case | What happened | Root cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 11 cases | NEWS2 partial instead of complete; missed RF-NEWS2-HIGH (H03), RF-NEWS2-MEDIUM and RF-SEPSIS (H06), and a lower NEWS2 band (H03) | Pulse written as `P 112` was not read. It had been excluded on purpose as ambiguous (G2P1). | v1.2.0: a capital `P` followed by a number counts as pulse only when another vital sign is in the same sentence. |
| 1 | H01 | Missed RF-ACS | `CP` shorthand for chest pain was not in the lexicon. | v1.2.0: capital `CP` reads as chest pain. |
| 2 | H29 | NEWS2 partial (6, medium) instead of complete (8, high) | `Resp 24` was not read as respiratory rate. | v1.3.0: `Resp` and `Resp.` are accepted. |
| 2 | H27 | Missed RF-REDUCED-FETAL-MOVEMENTS | "the baby has been moving much less than usual" was not in the lexicon. | v1.3.0: more phrasings of reduced movements. |

What this shows: every miss came from how clinicians write (shorthand and formats), not from the clinical thresholds. The safety layer generalises only as far as its vocabulary does, which is why the AI layer runs alongside it and why a larger, clinician-written held-out set is the next step before any real use.
