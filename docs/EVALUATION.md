### How the held-out sets were made

Both held-out sets were written by independent model instances that could read only the published criteria (SPEC §5.5 to §5.7). They had no access to the code, the lexicon, the tests or the development cases. Their expected labels are their own reading of the criteria. Per-case reasoning, including each NEWS2 parameter score, is in [`HOLDOUT-RATIONALE.md`](HOLDOUT-RATIONALE.md).

### Error analysis

| Step | Case | What happened | Root cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 11 cases | NEWS2 partial instead of complete; missed RF-NEWS2-HIGH (H03), RF-NEWS2-MEDIUM and RF-SEPSIS (H06), and a lower NEWS2 band (H03) | Pulse written as `P 112` was not read. It had been excluded on purpose as ambiguous (G2P1). | v1.2.0: a capital `P` followed by a number counts as pulse only when another vital sign is in the same sentence. |
| 1 | H01 | Missed RF-ACS | `CP` shorthand for chest pain was not in the lexicon. | v1.2.0: capital `CP` reads as chest pain. |
| 2 | H29 | NEWS2 partial (6, medium) instead of complete (8, high) | `Resp 24` was not read as respiratory rate. | v1.3.0: `Resp` and `Resp.` are accepted. |
| 2 | H27 | Missed RF-REDUCED-FETAL-MOVEMENTS | "the baby has been moving much less than usual" was not in the lexicon. | v1.3.0: more phrasings of reduced movements. |

What this shows: every miss came from how clinicians write (shorthand and formats), not from the clinical thresholds. The safety layer generalises only as far as its vocabulary does, which is why the AI layer runs alongside it and why a larger, clinician-written held-out set is the next step before any real use.

### AI layer: how this run was made and what it shows

All 56 cases (16 development and 40 held-out) were sent to the deployed app (`/api/analyze`) on 6 October 2026, prompt v1.2.0 and rules v1.3.0. This machine cannot reach the model provider, so the results were collected in a browser and imported with `scripts/eval-import.ts`. The rule columns are re-computed locally from the same rules version. On that day Gemini returned HTTP 503 (high demand) for `gemini-3.8-flash` and `gemini-3.7-flash` most of the time, so the fallback chain answered: `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` for 54 cases and `gemini-3.6-flash` for 2.

- **Every case returned all four outputs**, valid against the schema on the first call of the model that answered.
- **Quotes:** 433 of 436 AI quotes (99.3%) were found word for word in the case. The 3 that were not (C04, H16, H17) joined two separate parts of the text into one quote; the grounding check caught them and they are marked as unverified in the interface, which is the intended behaviour.
- **Coherence:** every rule red flag (65 of 65) has at least one next step linked to it.
- **Coverage after prompt v1.2.0:** a mean of 3.7 case-specific missing items and 5.1 next steps per case. With prompt v1.0.0 the same chest-pain case returned 1 missing item and 2 steps.
- **AI-added red flags (16):** all carry verified quotes. Most restate abnormal vital signs (tachycardia, hypotension, tachypnoea) or a feature next to a rule flag (unilateral leg swelling with RF-PE, meningism with RF-THUNDERCLAP); none contradicts a rule. When an AI flag repeats a rule flag it is dropped (C07: "presyncope in early pregnancy" under RF-PREGNANCY-PAIN-BLEEDING).
- **Latency:** median 6.4 s on the held-out set. The slowest cases (C06 32 s, C07 23 s) are the ones where several overloaded models were tried before one answered.

The 16 development-case outputs from this run are stored in `src/data/recordings.json`. They are what the "Use saved AI results" switch shows, and what the app falls back to automatically if every live model fails on a sample case.
